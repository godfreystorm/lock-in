The Lock-In Tree

Three roadmap.sh trees (Backend, AI Engineer, Data Structures & Algorithms) in one path. Duplicates are merged, and the "pick one" choices are made for your stack. Click any node to open its description and the free articles and videos roadmap.sh recommends. The status comes from this repo: a node turns Doing when its folder exists and Done after npm run done.
